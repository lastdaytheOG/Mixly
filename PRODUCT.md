# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and gamers on Windows whose system drive has run out of room. They're comfortable with paths and sizes, and they're usually in the middle of something else, so they want to see what's using space, move it, and get back to work.

## Product Purpose

Mixly frees up a full drive without breaking anything. It moves tools, games, apps and files between drives, clears what can be rebuilt, and can undo every move. Success is more free space, nothing broken, and no fear of clicking the button.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. It states facts plainly and shows exactly what will happen before it happens. Like Linear or Raycast: quiet, sharp and fast, with detail on screen rather than hidden behind wizards.

## Anti-references

PC cleaner software: red alerts, fake urgency, health scores, "your PC is at risk", and anything that pressures people into deleting things. A warning in Mixly is a fact with a next step, never a scare.

## Design Principles

- **Show the outcome first.** Say what will move, where to, and how much space it frees before anything changes.
- **Reversible by default.** Undo is always visible. Permanent actions are clearly separated and never the default.
- **Density over decoration.** Paths and sizes are the content. Every pixel should help someone decide what to move.
- **Never wait to see.** Show what's known immediately and fill in the rest as it arrives.
- **Say why.** When something can't be moved, say why in plain words.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow the Windows light or dark setting. Text meets WCAG AA contrast. Every action works from the keyboard, and status is never shown by color alone.
